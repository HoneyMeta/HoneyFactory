## When the AGPL is enough

You do not need a commercial license if you can comply with the AGPL. In practice that covers, for example:

- running an unmodified HoneyFactory in your own school, lab, training centre or plant;
- modifying HoneyFactory and offering the source code of your modified version to everyone who uses it over a network
  (AGPL section 13), under the AGPL;
- evaluation, teaching and research, including courses where students use a HoneyFactory server.

This page is a summary, not legal advice. The license text decides.

## When you need a commercial license

You need a commercial license if you want to do any of the following without the AGPL's obligations:

- ship HoneyFactory, or a product that contains or is derived from it (for example a training system, a
  commissioning test bench or a digital-twin product), to customers without releasing your changes and your combined
  work under the AGPL;
- offer HoneyFactory or a modified version as a hosted service (SaaS, an online teaching platform, a hosted virtual
  commissioning service) without offering the corresponding source code to its users;
- keep modifications or integrations (for example device models, plant templates, course packs, connectors) proprietary
  while they are combined with HoneyFactory;
- use HoneyFactory in an organisation whose policy does not allow AGPL software.
